Old Flurry v1.0.45 - Be Kind / Mod the Galaxy LED Billboard

Adds an eight-metre programmable LED billboard with a black display, bright LED pictures and green flashing words. The 44-second show displays Yoda (8s), a green leaf (8s), a TIE fighter firing green lasers toward an X-wing (12s), one Imperial emblem (8s), BE KIND (4s), then MOD THE GALAXY (4s). Picture holds are longer; the final word flashes remain unchanged.

Available from the QA console under City Decor as Be Kind - Mod the Galaxy LED Billboard. The standalone item uses native player-city decoration placement. A saved world copy is placed in Mos Eisley at the approved site, facing the corrected direction. No duplicate buildout or world copy is added by this client update.

Owner tested the display in the private classic client and supplied in-game footage. Broader hardware, city placement and later restart-retention testing remain QA.

Cumulative TRE-only content update for both existing 32-bit and 64-bit launchers. Close the game and click Update. No replacement launcher or server restart is needed. All earlier archives, login/preferences/profiles and x64 desktop-switching runtime 0.1.1 are preserved. Life Day remains disabled.
