Storming the Imperial Castle — public QA v1.0.31

Update through Old Flurry Assets, fully close and reopen the game, then speak with Princess Leia in Mos Eisley. Solo players and groups of up to eight can enter; the group leader should speak to Leia with everyone nearby and out of combat.

Includes the complete castle instance visuals: masonry walls and floors, crimson Imperial rugs, black seating, Star Destroyer consoles, Pillars of the Sith Ascendant, plants, stationary banners, enlarged fountains, stars and a red moon. The encounter includes Ben Kenobi's warnings and post-victory farewell, Oathguard waves, Darth Maul, sealed boss doors, repeat-entry/reset support, and rewards for the robe and a single-use fifth-generation double-bladed saber schematic. Player-installed crystals determine the crafted saber's blade colour. Existing Hoth, Halloween and earlier cumulative assets are retained.

Audio includes a looping Imperial March request throughout the instance and the supplied Ben voice cue on his first appearance. Please report whether music starts, repeats and stops on leaving; the latest explicit loop fix and black seating need QA confirmation.

Please test group balance using normal equipment and real buffs: group size, guard/boss damage, health, wipes, reset/re-entry, doors, reward delivery, inventory-full recovery, schematic use and crafting. Maul may remain unseen during his dialogue and appear when combat begins; this remains a known visual limitation. Crafting and all group-size combinations are not claimed fully tested.

TRE-only cumulative assets: four previous TREs retained, one castle TRE added. Configuration registration is included; no loose asset resources, credentials, profiles or executables are distributed. Later balance and visual patches can follow QA feedback.
